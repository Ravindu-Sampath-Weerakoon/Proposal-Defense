            \vspace{-1.5em}
            \begin{block}{Formal Research Gap Definition}
                \vspace{0.5em}
                \fontsize{8.5pt}{10.5pt}\selectfont
                The research gap is formally defined as the lack of a unified behavioral authentication framework that provides \textbf{provable non-invertibility} without sacrificing the \textbf{sub-second latency} required for continuous monitoring.
            \end{block}
            
            \fontsize{8.5pt}{10.5pt}\selectfont
            While existing studies achieve high accuracy using \ac{DL}, they fail to provide a mechanism for template revocability, meaning a compromised biometric profile is permanently lost. Conversely, cryptographic methods like \ac{HE} that provide privacy are too computationally expensive for real-time use on standard hardware.
            \vspace{0.5em}
            \begin{itemize}
                \setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
                \item \textbf{DL Models:} Lack template protection and revocability.
                \item \textbf{HE Methods:} High computational overhead and latency.
            \end{itemize}
