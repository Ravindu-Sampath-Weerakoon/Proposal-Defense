\section{LIMITATIONS AND CONSTRAINTS}

\begin{frame}{Assumptions and Technical Constraints}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Modality Limitation:} The system is currently restricted to desktop inputs (KMT) and does not include mobile-specific biometrics.
        \item \textbf{Data Availability:} Evaluation is bounded by the diversity and volume of data present in the selected benchmark datasets (BB-MAS and CMU).
        \item \textbf{Latency vs. Privacy Bound:} A critical constraint exists in the trade-off between projection dimension $k$ and system performance, targeting $<200$ms.
        \item \textbf{Behavioral Consistency:} Results assume that the primary user's typing and mouse usage patterns remain relatively stable during the study[cite: 273].
        \item \textbf{Environmental Noise:} Variations in hardware peripherals (different keyboards or mice) may introduce noise affecting the Equal Error Rate (EER)[cite: 283].
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
